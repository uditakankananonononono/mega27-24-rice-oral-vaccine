# 19A shared-protein table replay, 2026-09-27

The [2024 19A study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2024.1342662/full) supplies [supplementary files through Europe PMC](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10978600/supplementaryFiles). The archived 24-page PDF `PMC10978600_DataSheet_1.pdf` (SHA-256 `b08d51fe…`) ends with a shared-protein shotgun-MS table whose caption prints as "Sapplementary Table 5" in the text layer, listing salt-soluble proteins detected in both lines with aggregated protein-level PSM counts and printed 19A/WT ratios. The scripted layout-text replay checks 43 rows exactly: each printed ratio matches the 19A/WT PSM quotient to within 0.0005, and the AA, MW and pI pairs agree across the 19A and WT columns in every row. Two further printed rows carry defects and stay out of the strict replay: BAA77337.1's ratio is orphaned onto a separate line behind a fullwidth-comma artifact (6/2 matches the orphan 3.000), and BAD68706.l's 19A PSM cell is blank (printed ratio 3.000 with 3 WT PSMs implies 9; recorded, not filled in). Five accessions print with an internal space ("XP 015613091.1") and are normalized to underscore form.

Unlike the 51A supplement's complete 477-protein shared list, the printed 19A table covers only ratios ≥ 2.000 and stops at the document end with no filter stated in the caption, so it is a subset of shared proteins, not the full complement. On the 43 strict rows, raw PSM Pearson R² is 0.290017 and log(1+PSM) R² is 0.734762, far below the 51A raw 0.981503; PSM sums are 1182 versus 176 (ratio of sums 6.72). These are transparent descriptive results on aggregated published counts, not independent biology, a new discovery or a potency comparison; PSM abundance cannot be equated to antigen dose, immune efficacy or storage/digestion stability. Supplementary PDF, SHA-256, replay script, JSON and a hermetic test are in this repo. No accession-record, service, derivation or paper-page gate credit claimed.
