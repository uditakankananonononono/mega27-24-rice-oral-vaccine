# Published three-lot rice oral-antigen expression summary

The [2024 MucoRice 19A study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10978600/) reports SDS-PAGE densitometric antigen content for **three lots**: 19A at **4.94 ± 0.29 μg/mg** and 51A at **6.52 ± 0.22 μg/mg**. Arithmetic on the displayed means gives 19A/51A = **0.757669** and a difference of **1.58 μg/mg**. The source also describes three consecutive cultivation rounds with brown-rice yield based on six plants per round. Those plant samples measure yield, not six antigen-expression replicates. The XML archive, SHA-256, exact source-paragraph checks, derived mean arithmetic and replay test are in this repository.

This is a clearer production-lot expression lead than the older seed-metabolome proxy: a directly reported oral-vaccine antigen content, with lot-level summary context. It is not individual lot data, a fair controlled line comparison, an intestinal dose measure, an independent measured discovery or a benchmark. The meaning of the displayed ± values must be checked against the full statistical methods/figure before confidence intervals are computed; no SD/SEM assumption has been made. The paper and context still need secondary-source corroboration before any stronger inference. No service, accession, audited-derivation, page or judge gate credit is claimed.
