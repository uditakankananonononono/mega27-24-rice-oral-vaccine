# Two similar MucoRice trial registries are not interchangeable, 2026-09-28

The [published Japanese 60-participant phase I trial](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanmic/article/PIIS2666-5247%2820%2930196-8/fulltext) names registration `UMIN000018001`. Its [actual UMIN record, receipt R000020832](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000020832), describes three sequential 20-person cohorts, each randomized 10/10 to 1-, 3- or 6-g rice-product or placebo doses. A different, similarly titled [earlier UMIN000009688 record, receipt R000011211](https://upload.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000011211), targets only 20 participants and prints 750 mg rice powder containing 1 mg CTB. The latter must not be used to infer the dose composition or data availability of the published 60-person trial.

Both registry pages retain a results-publication field reading "Unpublished." For `UMIN000018001`, that field cannot be read literally as absence of a published trial because the original Lancet article exists and cites that ID. The archived HTML pages and replay script check exact record identifiers and dose fields. This is a source-identity warning only, not a new clinical finding, accession-payload analysis, comparator win or C24 gate credit.
