# Two rice CTB summaries: source-aligned reconciliation, not a replication

The [2021 51A seed-bank paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12864-020-07355-7) reports mean CTB concentration of 6.45 ± 0.89 μg/mg seed weight in the master seed bank (MSB) and 5.83 ± 0.58 μg/mg in the next seed bank (NSB), with author-reported P=0.60. Its comparison is between generations of *line 51A*. The [2024 19A paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2024.1342662/full) reports 4.94 ± 0.29 μg/mg in line 19A and 6.52 ± 0.22 μg/mg in line 51A, with n=3 lots for densitometry. Its comparison is between *lines*, under a different production setting. Both papers describe SDS-PAGE densitometric CTB quantification, but the corresponding sampling frames and laboratory contexts are not interchangeable.

Arithmetic on the published means only: 2021 NSB/MSB = 0.903876 and NSB minus MSB = -0.62 μg/mg; 2024 19A/51A = 0.757669 and 19A minus 51A = -1.58 μg/mg. The 2024 51A mean exceeds the 2021 MSB mean by only 0.07 μg/mg, but this numerical closeness is **not** evidence of equivalence, reproducibility, manufacturing stability or dose preservation. It is a cross-publication comparison of aggregates in different settings. The 2021 P=0.60 cannot establish equivalence. The archived primary XML passages do not resolve whether the displayed ± numbers are SD or SEM, and neither passage contains the individual measurements needed for a pooled variance or independent hypothesis test. Do not derive a confidence interval from these summaries or mistake seed-bank-generational variation for a 19A-vs-51A controlled comparison.

The script `scripts/reconcile_published_ctb_summaries.py` matches both source passages in archived full text, records source SHA-256 and quotes, replays only descriptive arithmetic, and emits `results/reconciled_published_ctb_summaries.json`; a test checks the replay. The live Europe PMC [2021 XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7814724/fullTextXML) matched the archived SHA-256 at this audit. This is a within-topic source-integrity and estimand clarification, not a biological discovery, strongest-comparator win, independent lot-level dataset, audited derivation credit, or completed paper page. Individual lot measurements, study comparability, dose/digestion endpoint and the larger project gates remain open.
