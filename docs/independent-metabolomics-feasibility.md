# Independent rice metabolomics source feasibility, 2026-09-26

Two independent published rice datasets were retrieved from MetaboLights, with original MAF and sample tables archived and SHA-256 checked:

- [MTBLS288](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS288), rice grain developmental stages, GC-MS assay: [31 MAF feature rows](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/ws/studies/MTBLS288/download?file=m_MTBLS288_dynamic_metabolomics_of_rice_grain_metabolite_profiling_mass_spectrometry_v2_maf.tsv), 81 sample rows. Compared with MTBLS437, exact normalized name strings overlap at six analytes and database identifiers at twelve. These are different comparability measures; repeated names, synonyms and processing matter.
- [MTBLS801](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS801), rice cultivar response to field heat/drought stress: [205 feature rows in split GC assay](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/ws/studies/MTBLS801/download?file=m_MTBLS801_Split_GC_mass_spectrometry_v2_maf.tsv) plus [706 in splitless GC assay](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/ws/studies/MTBLS801/download?file=m_MTBLS801_Splitless_GC_mass_spectrometry_v2_maf.tsv), 2,854 sample rows. Compared with MTBLS437, exact normalized names overlap at 22 analytes and database identifiers at 35. All counts are source-table rows/IDs, not independently fetched raw measurements or independent MucoRice cohorts.

`results/external_metabolomics_feasibility.json` reproduces the overlap and records hashes. These datasets have different biological questions, study protocols and groups; neither is an independent CTB51A-versus-parent validation cohort. Overlap is a lead for platform robustness work, not grounds to claim cross-study performance or an antigen-related discovery. The recent ChatGPT critique suggested held-out evaluation; this audit shows a narrower seed-metabolome robustness endpoint is possible to investigate, but whether actual measurements/units are harmonizable remains open. The project needs a preregistered same-task benchmark on an untouched cohort before any winner claim. No project gate closed.
