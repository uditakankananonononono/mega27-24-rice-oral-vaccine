# 51A Table 2 printed-ratio replay, 2026-09-28

The [2021 seed-bank article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7814724/) lists six aggregated PSM comparisons in Table 2. The script parses the archived article XML, takes each printed NSB/MSB PSM-count ratio, and checks whether division of the printed counts rounds to the displayed three decimals. All six do. Four ratios are below one and two above one; the ratio of summed NSB PSM to summed MSB PSM across these six selected rows is computed in the JSON. This is a print-layer arithmetic audit, not an independent measurement. The article's prose says the selected protein levels did not differ considerably; it does not supply individual seed-level uncertainty for this table. This check does not infer allergenicity, safety, antigen dose, immune response, or a benchmark win. Protein accession labels are copied from the table, not individually fetched records.
