# 2007 original-source antigen protection, bounded comparator

The [2007 original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1904174/) reports that an oral 50 mg rice-powder dose containing 75 µg CTB elicited fecal IgA in mice, while the same nominal 75 µg of purified recombinant CTB elicited no or very low fecal IgA. Its in-vitro experiment treated 10 mg seed powder containing 15 µg CTB and equal-dose purified CTB with pepsin at 0.5 mg/ml, pH 1.7, one hour at 37 °C; Figure 2 describes approximately 75% intact rice-expressed CTB versus no intact purified recombinant CTB. Figure 4 reports unchanged CTB content after room-temperature (25 °C) storage for 1.5 years, compared to fresh seed (29 ± 4 µg/seed), and comparable fecal IgA after immunization with stored rice. The two printed powder dose ratios both equal 1.5 µg CTB/mg powder.

This is a **published positive** nominal-dose comparator and storage observation. It is not our own comparator win or independent biological discovery. The approximate Figure 2 fraction is not a raw-replicate estimate; "not changed" is no prespecified equivalence test; the mouse result is not human protection. Crucially, the 2007 rice line is not identified as later production line 19A or 51A, and its 1.5 µg/mg ratio must not be pooled with the 2024 19A 4.94 µg/mg lot mean. The exact source HTML, excerpts, and arithmetic are replayed by `scripts/audit_2007_rice_protein_protection.py`, with hermetic test. Project gate credit remains zero.
