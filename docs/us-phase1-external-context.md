# Independent US MucoRice-CTB clinical abstract, 2026-09-28

The [PubMed record for the US phase I study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35484039/) (Vaccine 2022, DOI 10.1016/j.vaccine.2022.04.051) is a separate randomized, double-blind placebo-controlled trial. Its abstract states that the 6-g MucoRice-CTB dose was tested in healthy men and women, that 2 of 9 treated subjects had a salivary CTB-specific IgA response, and that antibodies from 3 of 5 responders blocked CTB binding to GM1. This offers a within-topic external clinical check on the 6-g rice-product dose assessed in the earlier [Japanese phase I trial](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanmic/article/PIIS2666-5247%2820%2930196-8/fulltext), which enrolled men only and assigned ten treated men to the 6-g cohort.

The US abstract does not report the dose's CTB mass, participant-level outcome values or a directly matched faecal IgA endpoint. Thus its 2/9 saliva measure must not be equated with the Japanese trial's faecal IgA measure; 3/5 is a responder-selected subset rather than all treated participants. No combined treatment effect, head-to-head comparator win, participant-level independent validation, discovery or project gate credit follows. `scripts/audit_us_trial_external_context.py` checks the archived PubMed Medline text against the archived Japanese full text; results are replayable in `results/us_trial_external_context.json`.
