# Rice oral-vaccine source-eligibility screen, 2026-09-26

Scope: published data only. This screen does not design constructs or optimize sequences and is not a biological efficacy claim.

- Original 2021 MucoRice-CTB 51A seed-bank comparison: [BMC Genomics full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7814724/), archived in `data/sources/pmc7814724.xml`. It compares three master-seed-bank and three next-seed-bank samples, detects 664 and 722 salt-soluble proteins respectively, 477 overlapping, and reports an R² of 0.982 for peptide-spectrum-match comparison. These are source claims, not newly replicated estimates. The manuscript links study DRA011151; its availability paragraph also says DRX011151, which appears inconsistent with the six DRX246636-641 experiment accessions returned by ENA. The discrepancy needs source-author clarification; do not silently correct it.
- [ENA study run report](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/filereport?accession=DRA011151&result=read_run&fields=run_accession,experiment_accession,sample_accession&format=tsv), archived in `data/sources/DRA011151_ena_runs.tsv`, lists six run, experiment and sample triples. This is metadata, not six individually fetched reads or 120 accession-level datasets. An identical source in NCBI SRA must not be double-counted as independent data.
- [2021 phase-1 human trial](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanmic/article/PIIS2666-5247%2820%2930196-8/fulltext) is a distinct safety/immunogenicity setting, not the 51A seed-bank proteomic repeat. Do not infer equivalence of manufacturing lot or dose from topic alone. Individual trial participant data access and strongest comparator remain to be established.

Research opportunity: test a source-identity/evidence reader against manually adjudicated original records, and seek independently accessioned numeric expression/stability outcomes before a novel biological assertion. No gate is crossed by this screen: 0/40 independently applied research services (retrieval and preliminary source inspection only), 0/120 individually fetched/used accession datasets, 0/10 audited derivations, 0 substantive paper pages, no tested comparator success or novelty.
