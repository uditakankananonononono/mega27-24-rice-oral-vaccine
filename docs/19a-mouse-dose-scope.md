# 19A oral mouse study: reported dose matches lot mean, schedule text differs, 2026-09-28

The [published 19A study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10978600/) prints a 150 mg rice-powder mouse dose containing 740 µg CTB in its methods. That ratio is **4.933 µg CTB per mg powder**, within rounding of its reported three-lot 19A SDS-PAGE densitometry mean, **4.94 µg/mg**. This is useful source-internal unit consistency, not an independently measured lot or individual exposure. The same methods say mice were immunized **four** times at two-week intervals, whereas the **Figure 6 caption** says **five** times at two-week intervals. Without source clarification, the administration count remains unresolved. Do not label the figure as Figure 4 or silently select one schedule.

This mouse finding is distinct from the [human phase I trial](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanmic/article/PIIS2666-5247%2820%2930196-8/fulltext), which separately reports four administrations of 1-, 3- or 6-g rice product. Its mouse dosing and 19A three-lot concentration cannot determine human delivered CTB or digestion survival. The archived article XML, exact quoted source paragraphs, arithmetic and a replay test establish only this bounded unit/schedule audit. No comparator, discovery or project gate credit.
