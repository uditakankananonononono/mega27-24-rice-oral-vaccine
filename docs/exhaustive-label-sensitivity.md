# Exhaustive four-versus-five assignment sensitivity, exploratory

The [published MTBLS437 metabolite table](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS437) contains nine samples in the selected contrast, four MR-CTB51A and five parental hydroponic Nipponbare. The prior identifier-deduped stability screen found 95/104 analytes with consistent raw/log mean-difference direction through leave-one-out deletion, and one of eight rotated-label controls reached 97. This follow-up enumerates **all 126** ways to assign four of nine sample positions to the first group. It applies the same identifier-keyed per-sample median collapse and the same direction criterion without changing the source or fitting a model.

The observed split gives **95** stable analytes, and **2 of 126 assignments** give at least 95; the maximum is 97 and the median is 55. These counts describe label sensitivity, **not a permutation p-value**. This exploratory procedure and contrast were chosen after seeing source outcomes, and randomization/exchangeability under technical batch and biological conditions was not established. An identical dataset can look stable under a misleading assignment. The statistic also does not assess biological mechanism, oral antigen dose, digestion or a held-out comparator. The script, full 126-count JSON, original table hash and hermetic replay test permit inspection; no gate credit is claimed.
