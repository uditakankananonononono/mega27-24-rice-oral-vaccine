# A verified ISEF exemplar, not a substitute for evidence

[Society for Science's Gordon E. Moore Awards history](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/awards/gordon-e-moore-awards/) identifies Natasha Kulviwat's 2023 project, "The Neurobiology of Suicide: Claudin-5 Is a Novel Biomarker of Suicide Pathogenesis." The [official finalist abstract](https://abstracts.societyforscience.org/Home/FullAbstract?AllAbstracts=False&Category=Biomedical+and+Health+Sciences&FairCountry=Any+Country&FairState=Any+State&ISEFYears=0%2C&ProjectId=23691) is the project record. This is a lesson in specifying a testable marker and evidence path, **not** an endorsement or comparator for MucoRice, and the illness, assays and validation design cannot be transposed to rice. For this project, insist on a named, measurable endpoint, independent data, a fair best comparator and uncertainty before any novelty claim.
