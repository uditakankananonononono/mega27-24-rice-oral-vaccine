# 19A supplementary Table 1: aggregate percentage mismatch

A source-integrity replay of [the 2024 study's original supplementary PDF](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10978600/supplementaryFiles), page 15, checks its printed transformation-frequency counts without proposing any construct or protocol change. The four individual ratios round to the percentages displayed: 15/53 to 28%, 17/36 to 47%, 15/53 to 28%, and 12/50 to 24%. The three count columns sum correctly across rows: 192 screened calli, 59 positive calli, and 48 positive T1 seeds. But the printed total percentage is **32%**, while **59/192 = 30.729%**, rounding to **31%**. This discrepancy is in the printed aggregate, not a challenge to the four row counts; the source does not establish why the total differs. The supplementary page was also inspected visually to confirm the values are not a text-extraction artifact.

The script replays the original PDF page, pins its SHA-256 in JSON, verifies row and total arithmetic, and is covered by a hermetic test. This is a useful source-quality correction, not independent biology, vaccine-dose inference, a comparator win, a new discovery, or credit for any of the locked project gates. The separate independent-lot-data search remains open; these are historical screening counts and cannot replace lot-level antigen measurements.
