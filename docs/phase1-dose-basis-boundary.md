# Human phase 1 dose basis versus seed-bank concentration, 2026-09-28

The [MucoRice-CTB phase 1 trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35544149/) describes randomization of 60 healthy adult men across three rice-product mass cohorts (1, 3, 6 g), each with 10 assigned vaccine and 10 assigned placebo, and four planned administrations per person. It reports two follow-up losses in the 3 g vaccine arm and one in the 6 g vaccine arm. The abstract does **not** give CTB content per administered gram of rice, individually measured trial seed-lot CTB concentrations, or a matched digestion-survival fraction. The full text **does** print intended CTB mass for each rice-product dose; see `docs/phase1-fulltext-content.md`. Even with those full-text cohort contents, the separately published [2021 seed-bank](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7814724/) and [2024 19A](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10978600/) mean CTB levels cannot be multiplied by the trial's gram doses and presented as measured participant antigen exposure. These are distinct source contexts. The script checks the PubMed abstract's source markers and allocation arithmetic, not individual records or trial results. No clinical protection inference, new comparator or gate credit.
