# Frozen before evaluating reference cases: dose-stage provenance checker

Question: can a source-scoped calculator avoid inappropriate cross-publication concentration transfer and stage conflation on the existing 2007, 2021, 2024 and human trial records? The tool is a designed prototype, not a newly validated algorithm. Inputs are typed claims (study, line, material, cohort/lot, stage, quantity and uncertainty status), with citations from archived source audits. Exact-source reported dose mass is preserved; only within the same study/line/product, same cohort or lot, same stage and compatible units may a directly measured concentration be multiplied by product mass. Unknown cohort/lot identity blocks inference. In vitro digestion fractions are never multiplied into oral delivered dose absent matched formulation, specimen and assay in the same experiment. Mouse schedule conflicts remain a set {4,5}, not a chosen value. Report refusals and missing measurements. Evaluate on a frozen, hand-adjudicated set of six cases, including tempting wrong cross-study and cross-stage transfers, and compare against a naive multiply-any-available-ratio baseline. Never call a source consistency check individual-dose validation; do not grant gate credit from synthetic/reference cases. Source records were seen before this prereg; performance is a transparent regression demo, not an untouched benchmark.
